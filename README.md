# Aetheris

AI-powered operational intelligence platform that predicts and prevents logistics failures before businesses lose revenue.

---

## Problem

Logistics companies face operational losses due to:
- delayed shipments
- warehouse bottlenecks
- escalation risks
- manual monitoring limitations
- customer dissatisfaction

Current systems are mostly reactive and fail to intelligently prevent operational failures before business impact occurs.

---

## Solution

Aetheris is an AI-powered operational intelligence platform that:
- monitors logistics workflows
- predicts operational risks
- detects escalation probability
- recommends corrective actions
- automates operational alerts
- provides conversational AI operational assistance

---

## Core Features

- Live Operations Dashboard
- AI Risk Detection
- Predictive Operational Alerts
- AI Operations Copilot
- Intelligent Recommendations
- Real-Time Shipment Monitoring
